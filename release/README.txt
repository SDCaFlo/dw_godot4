dw_godot4 - an unofficial, non-commercial Godot port of Digimon World (PlayStation)

This download contains no game content. You need your own copy of Digimon World,
US release (SLUS-01032), as a disc image: .cue + .bin (best), a single .bin, or .iso.

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (keep the tools folder next to dw_godot4.exe).
2. Start dw_godot4.exe. Windows may warn that the app is unrecognised (it isn't
   signed): "More info" > "Run anyway".
3. The first start asks for your disc image and converts the game content from it,
   once, on this computer (about 25 minutes). It is stored in
   %APPDATA%\dw_godot4\content\dw1; your saves go to %APPDATA%\dw_godot4\saves.
4. Then the game starts. Hold Shift while it starts for the launcher's settings
   (another image, convert again, the update check).

Updates: the launcher tells you when a new version is out. Unzip the new version
over this folder; your saves and the converted content are kept.

Movies are not converted yet. Do not share the converted content or disc images.
